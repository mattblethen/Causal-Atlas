# Research Standards

## Claim Discipline
- Do not turn a claim into settled note text until it has a source note or source link.
- Put uncertain or interpretive claims in `Research Notes`.
- Use `confidence: low` for unsupported, debated, or preliminary material.
- Use `confidence: medium` when reliable sources support the claim but interpretation remains open.
- Use `confidence: high` only for well-established claims supported by strong sources.

## Source Notes
Every source note should include:
- Source type
- Author or institution
- Title
- Publisher
- Publication date when available
- URL or full citation
- Access date for web sources
- Useful claims or evidence
- Connected notes

## Preferred Sources
- Primary documents and archival collections
- Government records
- University pages and academic publications
- Museums and libraries
- Recognized historical institutions
- Scholarly books or peer-reviewed articles

## Avoid
- Unsourced listicles
- SEO summaries
- AI-generated summaries
- Single-source explanations for debated causes
- Copying long passages instead of extracting short evidence notes

## Research Pass Pattern
1. Create or identify source notes.
2. Extract claims into source notes.
3. Draft or update event/force/actor notes.
4. Add backward and forward links.
5. Update logs and maps.
6. Record remaining questions in [[Research Queue]].
