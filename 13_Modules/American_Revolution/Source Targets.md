# American Revolution Source Targets

## Primary Source Targets
- Treaty texts and parliamentary acts connected to the Seven Years' War settlement.
- British budget, debt, and taxation records where available.
- Stamp Act text and colonial responses.
- Stamp Act Congress resolutions.
- Massachusetts Circular Letter, imperial response, and related assembly records.
- Nonimportation agreements, boycott broadsides, and homespun/Daughters of Liberty sources.
- Tea Act text and East India Company context.
- Coercive Acts text.
- Continental Congress records.
- Lexington and Concord contemporary accounts.
- Declaration of Independence drafts and final text.

## Institutional Source Targets
- Library of Congress
- National Archives
- Founders Online
- Avalon Project
- UK Parliament
- British Library
- Massachusetts Historical Society
- National Park Service

## Secondary Source Targets
- University-published summaries for event framing.
- Museum or library essays for source context.
- Scholarly books or articles for disputed causal interpretation.
