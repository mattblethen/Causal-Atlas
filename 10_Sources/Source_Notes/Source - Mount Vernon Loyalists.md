# Source - Mount Vernon Loyalists

## Bibliographic Info
George Washington's Mount Vernon digital encyclopedia article on Loyalists.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- Loyalists were colonists who supported Britain during the American Revolution, though exact numbers are uncertain.
- Loyalists came from many social classes, occupations, and regions.
- Their motives varied and could include ideology, caution, officeholding, local conflict, or opposition to Patriot tactics.
- The source estimates that 60,000 to 80,000 Americans left the country by 1783.
