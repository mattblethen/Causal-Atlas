# Source - Commonwealth Museum Calling for Troops

## Bibliographic Info
Commonwealth Museum exhibit page on the arrival and quartering of British soldiers in Boston.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- British soldiers came from Halifax, and Lord Hillsborough also ordered two regiments from Ireland.
- Quartering became an immediate controversy in a small colonial town.
- Some troops sheltered temporarily at Faneuil Hall and the Town House, while others camped on Boston Common.
- The former Manufactory House became a flashpoint when residents resisted orders to vacate for troop quartering.
