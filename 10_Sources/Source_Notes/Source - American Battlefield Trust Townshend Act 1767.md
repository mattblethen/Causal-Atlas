# Source - American Battlefield Trust Townshend Act 1767

## Bibliographic Info
Primary-source presentation of the Townshend Revenue Act text.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- The act placed duties on imported goods including glass, lead, paper, and tea.
- The law framed revenue as support for administration of justice, civil government, and defense in America.
- It linked customs duties, revenue, and imperial administration after the Stamp Act crisis.
