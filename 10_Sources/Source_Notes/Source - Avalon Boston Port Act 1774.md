# Source - Avalon Boston Port Act 1774

## Bibliographic Info
Text of the Boston Port Act, March 31, 1774, hosted by the Avalon Project at Yale Law School.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- The act explicitly refers to the destruction of East India Company tea as a reason for closing Boston's harbor.
- Beginning June 1, 1774, ordinary loading and unloading of goods in Boston Harbor was prohibited.
- The act used customs enforcement, forfeitures, and port closure as imperial punishment and leverage.
