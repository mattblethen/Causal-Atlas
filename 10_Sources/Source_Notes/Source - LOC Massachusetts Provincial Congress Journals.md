# Source - LOC Massachusetts Provincial Congress Journals

## Bibliographic Info
Digitized Library of Congress copy of the 1838 printed journals of the Massachusetts Provincial Congresses and Committee of Safety.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- The volume contains journals of the Massachusetts Provincial Congresses in 1774 and 1775.
- The title notes an appendix containing narratives of April 19, 1775 and related Revolutionary documents.
- Useful as a source base for Massachusetts Provincial Congress, Committee of Safety, militia mobilization, and Lexington and Concord.
