# Source - LOC Philipsburg Proclamation

## Bibliographic Info
Library of Congress record for the 1779 New York broadside issued by Sir Henry Clinton, commander in chief of British forces in North America.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- LOC identifies the item as a 1779 New York proclamation connected to Henry Clinton.
- The broadside is one page and available through LOC as online text, image, and PDF.
- The proclamation forbade claims over people enslaved by rebels who took refuge with the British army.
- It promised security inside British lines and the ability to follow an occupation there.

## Relevant Quotes
> "full Security to follow within these Lines"
