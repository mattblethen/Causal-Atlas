# Source - NPS Battle of Bunker Hill

## Bibliographic Info
National Park Service article on the Battle of Bunker Hill.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- The Battle of Bunker Hill took place on June 17, 1775, mostly on Breed's Hill in Charlestown.
- NPS identifies it as the first major battle of the American Revolution.
- The battle lasted about two hours and shocked the surrounding towns and colonies.
- It grew out of the siege of Boston and British attempts to control the heights around the town.
