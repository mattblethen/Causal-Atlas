# Source - Avalon Quebec Act 1774

## Bibliographic Info
Text of the Quebec Act, October 7, 1774, hosted by the Avalon Project at Yale Law School.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- The act provided a government framework for Quebec after the 1763 settlement.
- It addressed civil government, religion, law, and territory in formerly French Canada.
- In the American Revolution module, it matters because many colonists grouped it with the Intolerable Acts and read it through western land, religion, and imperial authority anxieties.

## Relevant Quotes
