# Source - NPS Siege of Boston Overview

## Bibliographic Info
National Park Service overview of the Siege of Boston.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- The siege lasted eleven months, from Lexington and Concord on April 19, 1775 to British evacuation on March 17, 1776.
- British forces were trapped in Boston while colonial forces surrounded the town.
- The siege included battles, skirmishes, raids, civilian hardship, scarce food and supplies, and contested harbor space.
- Dorchester Heights forced the British evacuation.
