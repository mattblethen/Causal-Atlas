# Source - Nova Scotia Archives Book of Negroes

## Bibliographic Info
Nova Scotia Archives presentation of the Book of Negroes within African Nova Scotians in the Age of Slavery and Abolition.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- Nova Scotia Archives identifies the Book of Negroes as a central document for African American immigration to Nova Scotia after the War of Independence.
- The register includes names and descriptions of about 3,000 Black refugees registered on vessels from New York to Nova Scotia between April 23 and November 30, 1783.
- The source notes that the book also records some cases of fugitive slaves returned to owners before they could leave.
- It explains that British and American versions were created separately at the same time.
- The British version is in the Sir Guy Carleton papers at the Public Record Office / National Archives of Great Britain.
