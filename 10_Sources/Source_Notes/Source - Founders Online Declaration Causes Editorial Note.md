# Source - Founders Online Declaration Causes Editorial Note

## Bibliographic Info
Founders Online/National Archives editorial note from the Papers of Thomas Jefferson on the Declaration of the Causes and Necessity for Taking Up Arms.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- Congress appointed a committee to prepare a declaration in June 1775, later adding John Dickinson and Thomas Jefferson.
- Dickinson produced the draft reported to Congress on July 6, which Congress approved the same day.
- The editorial note characterizes the final text as a collaboration between Jefferson and Dickinson rather than a simple radical text softened by a moderate.
- The note is useful for separating drafting history from later authorship claims.
