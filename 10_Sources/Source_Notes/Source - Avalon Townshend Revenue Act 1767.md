# Source - Avalon Townshend Revenue Act 1767

## Bibliographic Info
Avalon Project text of Great Britain Parliament's Townshend Revenue Act, effective November 20, 1767.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- The act said revenue was expedient for justice administration, civil government, defense, protection, and security in America.
- It imposed duties on imported glass, lead, painters' colors, tea, and many categories of paper.
- The act directed that duties be collected through existing customs procedures and kept separate in the Exchequer for colonial defense and administration.
- It authorized revenue to be applied to civil government and justice administration in the colonies.
- It extended customs reporting requirements and writs-of-assistance authority in the colonies.
