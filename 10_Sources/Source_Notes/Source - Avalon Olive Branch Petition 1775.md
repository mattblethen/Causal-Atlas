# Source - Avalon Olive Branch Petition 1775

## Bibliographic Info
Avalon Project transcription of the July 8, 1775 Journals of the Continental Congress entry containing the Petition to the King.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- The Journals entry records that the Petition to the King was engrossed, compared, and signed on July 8, 1775.
- The petition speaks in loyal-subject language while asking the king to use royal authority to relieve colonial grievances.
- The text shows Congress presenting armed defense and reconciliation as compatible in July 1775.
