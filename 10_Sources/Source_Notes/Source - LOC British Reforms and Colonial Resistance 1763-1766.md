# Source - LOC British Reforms and Colonial Resistance 1763-1766

## Bibliographic Info
Library of Congress primary source timeline section on British reforms and colonial resistance from 1763 to 1766.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- Britain's January 1763 national debt exceeded 122 million pounds.
- Annual interest on that debt exceeded 4.4 million pounds.
- The cost of victory and postwar order led British leaders to consider colonial revenue, tighter imperial control, a standing army in America, and western land restrictions.
- These policies planted conflicts between Britain and the colonies.
