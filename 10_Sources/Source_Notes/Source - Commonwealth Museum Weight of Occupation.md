# Source - Commonwealth Museum Weight of Occupation

## Bibliographic Info
Commonwealth Museum exhibit page on daily conditions during the occupation of Boston.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- The Massachusetts Council protested the deployment of troops by describing the occupation as if Boston were enemy territory.
- More than forty British soldiers deserted in the first two weeks, encouraged by townspeople.
- Private Richard Ames was executed for desertion on Boston Common.
- A guard post at Boston Neck checked people entering and leaving town.
- The exhibit estimates that one in three adult males in occupied Boston was a British soldier.
- Soldiers and townspeople clashed in streets, shops, taverns, and job markets.
