# Source - Avalon Massachusetts Circular Letter 1768

## Bibliographic Info
Avalon Project transcription of the February 11, 1768 Massachusetts Circular Letter to colonial legislatures.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- The Massachusetts House presented the Townshend duties as a shared problem for all colonies.
- The letter urged colonial assemblies to harmonize their constitutional responses.
- It accepted Parliament as the empire's supreme legislature but argued that constitutional limits still bound legislative power.
- It framed revenue duties on unrepresented colonists as infringements of natural and constitutional rights.
- It criticized Crown-paid governors, judges, civil officers, and customs officers as threats to colonial liberty.

## Relevant Quotes
> "harmonize with each other"
