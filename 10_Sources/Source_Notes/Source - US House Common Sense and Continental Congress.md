# Source - US House Common Sense and Continental Congress

## Bibliographic Info
U.S. House History, Art & Archives article on Common Sense and the Continental Congress.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- The article places the pamphlet in the second week of January 1776 and identifies Robert Bell as its Philadelphia printer.
- It notes that Common Sense was anonymous at publication and was later known as Paine's work.
- The article emphasizes rapid dissemination among the colonial population.
- Useful for linking the pamphlet's public argument to congressional movement toward independence.
