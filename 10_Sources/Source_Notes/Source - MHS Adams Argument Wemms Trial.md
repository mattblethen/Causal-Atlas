# Source - MHS Adams Argument Wemms Trial

## Bibliographic Info
Massachusetts Historical Society Adams Papers Digital Edition text of John Adams's defense argument in the soldiers' Boston Massacre trial, dated December 3-4, 1770.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- Adams appeared for the soldiers in Rex v. Wemms and framed the defense through legal standards rather than Patriot anger.
- He argued that homicide had to be sorted into murder, manslaughter, excusable killing, or justifiable self-defense.
- He emphasized the risk of punishing innocent defendants and cited English legal authorities to support careful jury judgment.
- The argument shows Enlightenment and common-law language operating inside a revolutionary public crisis.

## Relevant Quotes
