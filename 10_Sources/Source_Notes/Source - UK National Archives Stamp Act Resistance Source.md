# Source - UK National Archives Stamp Act Resistance Source

## Bibliographic Info
Extract from the Boston Evening Post, October 28, 1765, preserved in UK Colonial Office records.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- Colonial newspaper criticism framed the Stamp Act as a rights and consent problem, not only a tax burden.
- The source illustrates how print culture carried resistance arguments.
- The National Archives identifies the article as criticism of the Stamp Act before stamps arrived in the colonies.

## Relevant Quotes
> "Life, Liberty, and Property"
