# Source - MHS Boston Pamphlet 1772

## Bibliographic Info
Massachusetts Historical Society item page for the 1772 Boston Pamphlet, printed by Edes and Gill and T. and J. Fleet.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- The pamphlet collected statements and lists of perceived infringements and violations of colonists' rights.
- It grew from the work of the recently formed Boston Committee of Correspondence.
- The committee held its first meeting on November 3, 1772.
- A subcommittee chaired by James Otis prepared a rights statement.
- Six hundred copies were published and distributed to Massachusetts towns and districts, and to Boston clergy and selectmen.
