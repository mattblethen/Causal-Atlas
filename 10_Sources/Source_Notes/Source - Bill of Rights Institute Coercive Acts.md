# Source - Bill of Rights Institute Coercive Acts

## Bibliographic Info
Primary-source presentation of the Coercive/Intolerable Acts, including the Boston Port Act text.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- Parliament passed the Coercive Acts in 1774 as punishment for the Boston Tea Party and broader colonial resistance.
- The Boston Port Act closed Boston's port to ordinary commerce until imperial demands were met.
- The acts intensified colonial perception that Parliament threatened traditional rights and self-government.
