# Source - Britannica Nonimportation Agreements

## Bibliographic Info
Encyclopaedia Britannica overview of nonimportation agreements in American colonial history.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- Nonimportation agreements ran from the Stamp Act crisis through the imperial crisis before independence.
- They attempted to force British recognition of colonial political rights through economic pressure.
- Sons of Liberty and Whig merchants organized colonial nonimportation associations in response to the Stamp Act and Townshend Acts.
- British merchants and manufacturers faced reduced colonial trade and were expected to pressure Parliament.
- After the Intolerable Acts, the First Continental Congress provided for nonimportation, nonexportation, and committees.
