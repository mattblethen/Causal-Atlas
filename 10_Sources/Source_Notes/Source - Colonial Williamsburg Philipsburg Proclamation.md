# Source - Colonial Williamsburg Philipsburg Proclamation

## Bibliographic Info
Colonial Williamsburg Foundation, Our American Revolution page on the Philipsburg Proclamation of June 30, 1779.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- The page frames Clinton's 1779 proclamation as a broader policy than Dunmore's 1775 Virginia proclamation.
- It explains that Clinton's order promised security to rebel-owned enslaved people who reached British lines and did not require them to serve as soldiers.
- It treats the proclamation as official British policy across North America because Clinton was commander in chief.
- It connects Clinton's policy to Carleton's 1783 refusal to return many formerly enslaved people who had reached British lines before the preliminary peace cutoff.
- It estimates that almost 4,000 formerly enslaved people left New York with the British in 1783.
