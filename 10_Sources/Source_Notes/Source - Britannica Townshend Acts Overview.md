# Source - Britannica Townshend Acts Overview

## Bibliographic Info
Encyclopaedia Britannica overview of the Townshend Acts, revised January 13, 2026.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- Britannica dates the Townshend Acts to June 15-July 2, 1767.
- It describes the program as four acts tied to Parliament's authority, New York's assembly suspension, revenue duties, customs collection machinery, and the Indemnity Act.
- It identifies duties on lead, glass, paper, paint, and tea as colonial-port duties intended to raise revenue.
- It connects enforcement to customs officers, searchers, coast guard vessels, writs of assistance, and a Board of Customs Commissioners in Boston.
- It states that the 1770 repeal removed all revenue duties except the tea duty.

## Relevant Quotes
> "strict provisions for the collection of revenue duties"
