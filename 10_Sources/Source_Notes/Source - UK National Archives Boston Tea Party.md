# Source - UK National Archives Boston Tea Party

## Bibliographic Info
The National Archives (UK) lesson and source set on the Boston Tea Party, including British-side documents.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- On December 16, 1773, colonists destroyed 340 chests of East India Company tea in Boston Harbor.
- The protest targeted the tea tax and the East India Company monopoly created by imperial policy.
- The Tea Act was passed in spring 1773 to help the financially distressed East India Company.
- Parliament responded with Coercive Acts, which colonists labeled Intolerable Acts.
