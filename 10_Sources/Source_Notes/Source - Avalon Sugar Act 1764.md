# Source - Avalon Sugar Act 1764

## Bibliographic Info
Text of the Sugar Act of 1764 hosted by the Avalon Project at Yale Law School.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- The act imposed and revised duties on colonial imports including molasses and other goods.
- The text explicitly connects revenue to defending, protecting, and securing colonies and plantations.
- It strengthened customs enforcement and anti-smuggling regulation in the Atlantic trade system.
