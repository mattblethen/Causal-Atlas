# Source - NARA Native Americans and American Revolution

## Bibliographic Info
National Archives Museum exhibit page on Native Americans and the American Revolution.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- The Revolution was a watershed event for Indigenous people.
- The war affected roughly 250,000 Native Americans in British North America and more than 80 tribes east of the Mississippi River.
- Many Indigenous nations sought to preserve lands and sovereignty, regardless of which side they chose or whether they remained neutral.
- Most Native Americans saw losses during and after the Revolution.
