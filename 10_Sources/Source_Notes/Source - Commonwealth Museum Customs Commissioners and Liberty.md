# Source - Commonwealth Museum Customs Commissioners and Liberty

## Bibliographic Info
Commonwealth Museum exhibit page on customs commissioners, HMS Romney, impressment, and the seizure of John Hancock's sloop Liberty.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- Boston became the North American home base for British customs officials.
- Five customs commissioners arrived in Boston in November 1767.
- HMS Romney arrived on May 17, 1768, marking the beginning of British military involvement in customs suppression.
- Romney impressed seamen from vessels entering Boston Harbor, increasing tension and economic strain.
- Customs officers seized John Hancock's sloop Liberty and moved it toward Romney.
