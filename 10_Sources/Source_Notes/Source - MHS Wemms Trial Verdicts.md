# Source - MHS Wemms Trial Verdicts

## Bibliographic Info
Massachusetts Historical Society Adams Papers Digital Edition text of the verdicts in the soldiers' Boston Massacre trial, dated December 5, 1770.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- The jury acquitted William Wemms, James Hartegan, William McCauley, Hugh White, William Warren, and John Carrol.
- The jury found Matthew Killroy and Hugh Montgomery not guilty of murder but guilty of manslaughter.
- The document records that Killroy and Montgomery prayed benefit of clergy, were allowed it, and were discharged after branding.
- An editorial note specifies that the branding occurred on December 14, 1770.

## Relevant Quotes
> "not guilty of murder, but guilty of manslaughter"
