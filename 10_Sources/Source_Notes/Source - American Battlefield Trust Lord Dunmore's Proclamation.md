# Source - American Battlefield Trust Lord Dunmore's Proclamation

## Bibliographic Info
American Battlefield Trust primary-source presentation of Lord Dunmore's November 7, 1775 proclamation.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- Dunmore declared martial law in Virginia.
- The proclamation offered freedom to enslaved people held by rebels if they were able and willing to bear arms and join British troops.
- The proclamation connected slavery, military manpower, loyalty, rebellion, and British imperial strategy.

## Relevant Quotes
> "free that are able and willing"
