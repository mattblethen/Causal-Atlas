# Source - NPS Rebellion Minute Man

## Bibliographic Info
National Park Service Minute Man page on the April 1775 rebellion context.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- General Thomas Gage initiated a plan to deprive rebellious provincials of military capacity.
- Gage dispatched more than 800 British regulars toward Concord on April 18, 1775.
- His orders referenced ammunition, provisions, artillery, tents, small arms, and military stores collected at Concord.
