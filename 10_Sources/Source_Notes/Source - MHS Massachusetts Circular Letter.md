# Source - MHS Massachusetts Circular Letter

## Bibliographic Info
Massachusetts Historical Society collection entry for the published circular letter from the Journals of the Massachusetts House of Representatives.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- The collection identifies the document as a published letter from the Massachusetts House of Representatives journals.
- MHS attributes the letter to Samuel Adams and James Otis.
- The letter was sent to officials of other colonies in protest of the Townshend Acts.
- MHS summarizes the document as addressing the injustice of taxing colonists who were not represented in Parliament.

## Relevant Quotes
> "not represented in Parliament"
