# Source - NPS Concord North Bridge

## Bibliographic Info
National Park Service place page for Concord's North Bridge at Minute Man National Historical Park.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- Concord's North Bridge is where colonial minute men and militia were ordered to fire on British soldiers.
- The page states that British soldiers died there, making it a clear escalation in armed conflict.
- The British expedition to Concord was intended to seize and destroy military stores and equipment.
