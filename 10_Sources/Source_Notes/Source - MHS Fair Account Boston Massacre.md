# Source - MHS Fair Account Boston Massacre

## Bibliographic Info
Massachusetts Historical Society presentation of A Fair Account of the Late Unhappy Disturbance at Boston in New England, a 1770 London pamphlet.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- The pamphlet was written and compiled by Francis Maseres and Lieutenant Colonel William Dalrymple.
- It included a narrative and 31 testimonies intended to exonerate the soldiers and promote the Loyalist cause.
- MHS contrasts it with A Short Narrative of the Horrid Massacre in Boston, which favored the Patriot view.
- Both sides acted quickly to shape blame and influence opinion after March 5, 1770.
- The MHS commentary emphasizes contradictions among depositions and the difficulty of reconstructing the exact event.
