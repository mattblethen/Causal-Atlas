# Source - LAC Book of Negroes 1783

## Bibliographic Info
Library and Archives Canada database guide for the Carleton Papers - Book of Negroes, 1783.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- LAC explains that Carleton managed the orderly evacuation of New York after the preliminary peace treaty.
- The Book of Negroes recorded Black people embarking from New York, partly to address American complaints and possible compensation claims.
- LAC states that almost 3,000 names were recorded by the end of the evacuation.
- The database provides 2,831 references, one for each Black man, woman, or child listed.
- Entries can include name, age, gender, physical characteristics, occupation, military service, origin, former and current legal status, former owners, destination, ship, shipmaster, and inspection date.
