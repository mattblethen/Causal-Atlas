# Source - LOC Short Narrative of the Boston Massacre

## Bibliographic Info
Library of Congress digitized copy of a Boston town account of the Boston Massacre, originally prepared by James Bowdoin, Joseph Warren, and Samuel Pemberton.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- The source preserves a colonial account of the March 5, 1770 violence in Boston.
- Its publication history shows how the event was shaped into a public political narrative.
- The title itself frames the event as a massacre by British soldiers, showing the source's colonial perspective.
