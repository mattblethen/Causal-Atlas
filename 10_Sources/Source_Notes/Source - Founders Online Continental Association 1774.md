# Source - Founders Online Continental Association 1774

## Bibliographic Info
Founders Online/National Archives edition of the Continental Association adopted by the First Continental Congress on October 20, 1774.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- The Association framed post-1763 imperial administration as a connected system of grievances.
- It proposed nonimportation, nonconsumption, and later nonexportation as a peaceable pressure measure.
- It included a discontinuation of the slave trade in Article Two, while not ending slavery itself.
- It required local committees in counties, cities, and towns to observe conduct under the Association.
- It directed committees of correspondence to inspect custom house entries and share information.
