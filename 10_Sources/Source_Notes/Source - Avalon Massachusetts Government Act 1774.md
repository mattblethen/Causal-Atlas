# Source - Avalon Massachusetts Government Act 1774

## Bibliographic Info
Text of the Massachusetts Government Act, May 20, 1774, hosted by the Avalon Project at Yale Law School.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- The act changed Massachusetts government by replacing elected council structures with royal appointment.
- It increased the governor's authority over officials and courts.
- It restricted town meetings without governor approval, except for narrow electoral purposes.
- The act made parliamentary sovereignty visible as a direct alteration of colonial constitutional practice.
