# Source - National Archives Olive Branch Petition

## Bibliographic Info
National Archives Museum exhibit page on the Olive Branch Petition.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- Congress was divided in 1775 between those wanting to remain in the empire and those seeing separation as unavoidable.
- King George III did not formally receive or respond to the petition.
- The August 23, 1775 proclamation declaring the colonies in rebellion effectively answered the petition.
- The failed petition strengthened colonial support for independence.
