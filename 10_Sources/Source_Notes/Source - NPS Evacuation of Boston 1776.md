# Source - NPS Evacuation of Boston 1776

## Bibliographic Info
National Park Service article on the British evacuation of Boston in 1776.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- British forces evacuated Boston on March 17, 1776.
- Dorchester Heights and American fortifications made the British position untenable.
- The evacuation sent British troops and Loyalist refugees to Halifax, Nova Scotia.
- It marked a major early Patriot military success before the Declaration of Independence.
