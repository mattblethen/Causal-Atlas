# Source - LOC American Revolution Primary Source Timeline

## Bibliographic Info
Library of Congress classroom primary source timeline for the American Revolution, 1763-1783.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- The Seven Years' War changed the relationship between Britain and its North American colonies.
- British victory brought major costs, including war debt that shaped policies in the next decade.
- British efforts to reform colonial administration, enforce tax laws, and station troops in America created direct conflict with colonists.
- By the mid-1770s, the relationship between colonists and British authorities had become deeply strained.

## Relevant Quotes
> "The Seven Years' War changed everything."
