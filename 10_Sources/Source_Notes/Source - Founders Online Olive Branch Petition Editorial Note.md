# Source - Founders Online Olive Branch Petition Editorial Note

## Bibliographic Info
Founders Online/National Archives editorial note from the Papers of Benjamin Franklin on the Olive Branch Petition.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- Congress appointed a five-member committee including Franklin to draft a final appeal to the king.
- The submitted draft was the work of John Dickinson.
- Congress adopted the petition on July 8, 1775 with minimal amendment, and members signed it as individuals.
- The petition went unanswered, making it useful evidence for the failed-reconciliation branch point.
