# Source - LOC No Taxation Without Representation

## Bibliographic Info
Library of Congress exhibition page connecting Magna Carta, colonial rights claims, the Stamp Act crisis, and the Stamp Act Congress.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- The Stamp Act placed duties on printed materials and legal documents and broadened vice-admiralty jurisdiction.
- Delegates from nine colonies met in New York in October 1765 after the Massachusetts Assembly suggested intercolonial consultation.
- The Stamp Act Congress sought repeal through petitions to the king and Parliament.
- The congress adopted a thirteen-point rights statement centered on consent to taxation and the rights of Englishmen.
- John Dickinson was a major contributor to the Declaration of Rights and Grievances.
