# Source - MHS First Continental Congress Association Enforcement

## Bibliographic Info
Massachusetts Historical Society exhibit page on the First Continental Congress.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- MHS frames the Boston Port Bill and Coercive Acts as the context for the First Continental Congress.
- The Congress endorsed the Continental Association before adjourning in October 1774.
- MHS notes that Congress's proceedings were published after adjournment as pamphlets and broadsides.
- Loyalists denounced congressional measures, while advocates defended Congress.
- MHS states that thousands of colonists formed local committees to enforce the Association.
