# Source - Smithsonian Common Sense Pamphlet 1776

## Bibliographic Info
Smithsonian National Museum of American History collection record for a 1776 Common Sense pamphlet.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- The Smithsonian identifies the object as a 1776 pamphlet of Common Sense.
- The museum describes the pamphlet as an argument against monarchy and hereditary privilege and for American independence.
- The record notes that it first appeared anonymously in Philadelphia in January 1776.
- The Smithsonian states that twenty-five print editions appeared within a year.
