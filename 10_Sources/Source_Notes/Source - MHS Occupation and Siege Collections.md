# Source - MHS Occupation and Siege Collections

## Bibliographic Info
Massachusetts Historical Society collection guide overviewing materials related to the British occupation and siege of Boston, 1768-1776.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- MHS identifies its collections as containing manuscript and print material about the occupation and siege of Boston.
- The guide frames the occupation and siege as events that can be studied through pro-British sources, pro-American sources, and secondary sources.
- The guide explicitly covers the British occupation of Boston from 1768 to 1775 and the siege from 1775 to 1776.
- It lists diaries, letters, orderly books, public records, maps, graphics, and personal accounts useful for deeper research.
