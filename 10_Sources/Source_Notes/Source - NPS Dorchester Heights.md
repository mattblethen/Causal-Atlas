# Source - NPS Dorchester Heights

## Bibliographic Info
National Park Service page on Dorchester Heights at Boston National Historical Park.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- Dorchester Heights was fortified on the evening of March 4, 1776.
- Washington ordered continental forces and local volunteers to fortify the heights with cannon.
- The operation led British forces to evacuate Boston on March 17, 1776.
