# Source - Commonwealth Museum Occupation Landing

## Bibliographic Info
Commonwealth Museum exhibit overview page on the occupation of Boston.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- On September 28, 1768, eight British warships entered Boston Harbor and joined six already present.
- The next troop landing included the 14th and 29th Regiments.
- The 29th Regiment camped on Boston Common after parading past the Town House.
- The exhibit frames Paul Revere's depiction of the British landing as propaganda emphasizing an "insolent parade."
