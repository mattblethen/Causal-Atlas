# Source - MHS Samuel Adams James Warren Committee 1772

## Bibliographic Info
Massachusetts Historical Society item page for Samuel Adams's November 4, 1772 letter to James Warren in the Warren-Adams papers.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- The letter came immediately after Boston established its Committee of Correspondence.
- MHS frames the letter as Adams urging Warren that other towns must establish their own committees if Boston's committee was to succeed.
- The committee formed in response to the Crown's plan to pay judges' salaries from customs revenue.
- The Boston selectmen voted on November 2, 1772 to establish the Committee of Correspondence.
- MHS identifies the committee as a twenty-one-member body with ties to Boston political, merchant, legal, and medical circles.

## Relevant Quotes
> "other towns throughout the colony must follow suit"
