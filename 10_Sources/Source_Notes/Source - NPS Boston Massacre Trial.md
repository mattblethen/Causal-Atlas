# Source - NPS Boston Massacre Trial

## Bibliographic Info
National Park Service article on the trials of Captain Thomas Preston and the British soldiers after the Boston Massacre.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- British soldiers fired into a crowd on March 5, 1770, and five civilians died.
- Captain Thomas Preston and eight soldiers were arrested and jailed.
- John Adams agreed to help defend Preston and the soldiers because he believed accused people deserved counsel and a fair trial.
- Preston was found not guilty on October 30, 1770.
- Six soldiers were acquitted; Hugh Montgomery and Matthew Kilroy were convicted of manslaughter.
- The trials demonstrated colonial capacity for local legal process but did not prevent later British distrust and coercive legislation.
