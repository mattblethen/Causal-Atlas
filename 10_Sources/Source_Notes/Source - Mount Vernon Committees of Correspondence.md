# Source - Mount Vernon Committees of Correspondence

## Bibliographic Info
Mount Vernon digital encyclopedia article on committees of correspondence and their Boston-Massachusetts, intercolonial, and post-Coercive Acts systems.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- Mount Vernon distinguishes three committee systems: Boston-Massachusetts, intercolonial, and post-Coercive Acts.
- The intercolonial system emerged from Virginia's response to the Gaspee Affair investigation.
- By early 1774, all thirteen colonies except Pennsylvania had an intercolonial committee of correspondence.
- The post-Coercive Acts system helped choose delegates to the First Continental Congress.
- The Continental Association promoted local committees to enforce nonimportation and nonexportation.
