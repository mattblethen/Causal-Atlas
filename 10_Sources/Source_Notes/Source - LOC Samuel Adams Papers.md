# Source - LOC Samuel Adams Papers

## Bibliographic Info
Library of Congress collection record for the Samuel Adams papers.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- The collection includes correspondence, speeches, petitions, committee records, and other papers relating chiefly to Adams's political life in Massachusetts.
- Correspondents include John Adams, Thomas Jefferson, Arthur Lee, Richard Henry Lee, Thomas Paine, and James Warren.
- Useful for Samuel Adams, committees, Massachusetts resistance, and Patriot communication networks.
