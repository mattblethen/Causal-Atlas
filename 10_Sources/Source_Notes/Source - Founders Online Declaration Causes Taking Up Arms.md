# Source - Founders Online Declaration Causes Taking Up Arms

## Bibliographic Info
Founders Online/National Archives transcription of the Declaration of the Causes and Necessity for Taking Up Arms as adopted by Congress on July 6, 1775.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- Founders Online identifies the adopted text as a Continental Congress document dated July 6, 1775.
- The declaration justified armed resistance as a response to coercion and military violence.
- The adopted text still denied an immediate design to dissolve the imperial union or establish independent states.
- Founders Online notes early newspaper and pamphlet printings, useful for later print-circulation work.

## Relevant Quotes
> "Our cause is just."
