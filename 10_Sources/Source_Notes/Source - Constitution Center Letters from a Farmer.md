# Source - Constitution Center Letters from a Farmer

## Bibliographic Info
National Constitution Center educational source packet with excerpts and historical framing for John Dickinson's Letters from a Farmer in Pennsylvania.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- The letters were originally published in 1768 to protest a new wave of British taxes.
- Dickinson argued against the Townshend duties and helped clarify the colonial constitutional argument over revenue taxation.
- The source frames the letters as pushing colonists toward broader rejection of parliamentary taxation without representation.
- Dickinson distinguished trade regulation from revenue taxation and treated the revenue purpose as the constitutional danger.

## Relevant Quotes
> "No taxation without representation"
