# Source - Avalon Quartering Act 1774

## Bibliographic Info
Text of the Quartering Act of June 2, 1774, hosted by the Avalon Project at Yale Law School.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- The act addressed quartering British officers and soldiers in North America.
- It authorized colonial officials to arrange quarters beyond barracks when needed.
- The act connected imperial military presence to colonial governance and enforcement.

## Relevant Quotes
> "suitable quarters for officers and soldiers"
