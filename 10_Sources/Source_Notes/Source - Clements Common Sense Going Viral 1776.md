# Source - Clements Common Sense Going Viral 1776

## Bibliographic Info
University of Michigan William L. Clements Library online exhibit page on Common Sense print circulation.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- Clements frames Common Sense as a print phenomenon that spread rapidly in 1776.
- The first edition was printed by Robert Bell on January 10, 1776 without Paine's name on the pamphlet.
- Clements reports an initial print run of 1,000 copies and describes the first edition as selling quickly.
- The page explains the Paine-Bell printing dispute, later Bradford editions, London reprint censorship, and later French translation.
