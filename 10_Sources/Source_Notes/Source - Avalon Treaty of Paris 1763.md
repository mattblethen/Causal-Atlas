# Source - Avalon Treaty of Paris 1763

## Bibliographic Info
Text of the 1763 Treaty of Paris hosted by the Avalon Project at Yale Law School.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- The treaty formally concluded peace among Britain, France, Spain, and Portugal after the global conflict.
- The treaty confirms the war's multinational and imperial scale.
- The treaty settlement reshaped North American imperial control and created the setting for postwar British administration.

## Relevant Quotes
> "Done at Paris the tenth day of February, 1763."
