# Source - NCpedia Committees of Observation

## Bibliographic Info
NCpedia encyclopedia article on committees of observation in North Carolina.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- Committees of observation watched citizens reluctant to support the revolutionary movement.
- In Halifax County, committee members questioned merchant Andrew Miller after he refused to sign an Association against trade with British subjects.
- Miller explained that he held property belonging to someone in England.
- The committee then declared that members would not purchase goods from Miller.
