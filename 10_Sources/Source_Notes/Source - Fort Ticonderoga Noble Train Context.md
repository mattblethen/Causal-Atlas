# Source - Fort Ticonderoga Noble Train Context

## Bibliographic Info
Fort Ticonderoga Museum article contextualizing Henry Knox's movement of artillery from Ticonderoga to Boston.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- Knox reached Fort Ticonderoga on December 5, 1775.
- The artillery shortage was a critical problem for American forces besieging Boston.
- Nearly 60 tons of artillery were transported from the Champlain Valley toward the Boston siege lines.
- The captured artillery helped make Dorchester Heights and the evacuation of Boston possible.
