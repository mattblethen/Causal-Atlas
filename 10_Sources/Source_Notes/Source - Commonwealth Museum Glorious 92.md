# Source - Commonwealth Museum Glorious 92

## Bibliographic Info
Commonwealth Museum exhibit page on the Massachusetts Circular Letter and the "Glorious 92."

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- The Massachusetts House sent a circular letter to other colonies on February 11, 1768 urging united protest of the Townshend duties.
- Lord Hillsborough demanded that the circular letter be rescinded and implied a possible military response.
- The Massachusetts House rejected the demand by a vote of 92 to 17.
- The "Glorious Ninety Two" were celebrated by resistance supporters.
- Governor Bernard closed the legislative session in response.
