# Source - American Battlefield Trust Declaratory Act 1766

## Bibliographic Info
Primary-source presentation of the Declaratory Act of March 18, 1766.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- Parliament asserted that the colonies were subordinate to the crown and Parliament.
- The act claimed parliamentary power to bind the colonies "in all cases whatsoever."
- It accompanied repeal of the Stamp Act, preserving the sovereignty claim even while removing the tax.

## Relevant Quotes
> "in all cases whatsoever"
