# Source - NPS Declaration Causes Taking Up Arms

## Bibliographic Info
National Park Service article on the Declaration of the Causes of and Necessity of Taking Up Arms.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- The declaration was drafted in July 1775 and adopted by the Second Continental Congress in Independence Hall.
- It articulated why the colonies believed they had been compelled to take up arms.
- NPS identifies John Dickinson and Thomas Jefferson, along with other delegates, as important figures in shaping the declaration.
- The article frames the declaration as an early step on the road to independence.

## Relevant Quotes
