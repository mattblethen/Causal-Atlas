# Source - US House King's Proclamation for Suppressing Rebellion

## Bibliographic Info
U.S. House History, Art & Archives record for George III's August 23, 1775 proclamation.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- George III proclaimed the colonies in rebellion after the early battles of the Revolution.
- The document belongs to the Papers of the Continental Congress, 1774-1789.
- It is a primary communication showing the British state interpreting colonial resistance as rebellion rather than negotiation.
