# Source - MHS Legal Papers Boston Massacre Trials

## Bibliographic Info
Massachusetts Historical Society Adams Papers Digital Edition page for Legal Papers of John Adams, volume 3, including the Boston Massacre trial materials for Rex v. Preston and Rex v. Wemms.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- MHS organizes the Boston Massacre trial materials as criminal-law cases in John Adams's legal papers.
- The volume separates Captain Preston's trial from the soldiers' trial, Rex v. Wemms.
- The document list preserves preliminary arguments, crown evidence, defense evidence, jury charges, Adams's argument, and verdict materials.
- The source is useful for keeping the Massacre aftermath divided into propaganda, legal process, and verdict outcomes.
