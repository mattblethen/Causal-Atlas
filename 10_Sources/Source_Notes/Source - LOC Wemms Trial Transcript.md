# Source - LOC Wemms Trial Transcript

## Bibliographic Info
Library of Congress digitized 1824 Boston printing of the trial of the British soldiers of the 29th Regiment for the deaths of Crispus Attucks, Samuel Gray, Samuel Maverick, James Caldwell, and Patrick Carr. LOC notes that the first edition appeared in Boston in 1770 under the title The trial of William Wemms, James Hartegan, and others, taken in shorthand by John Hodgson.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- The LOC record identifies the soldiers' trial as held at Boston by adjournment on November 27, 1770.
- The defendants included William Wemms, James Hartegan, William McCauley, Hugh White, Matthew Killroy, William Warren, John Carrol, and Hugh Montgomery.
- The victims named in the title are Crispus Attucks, Samuel Gray, Samuel Maverick, James Caldwell, and Patrick Carr.
- The item contains 146 pages and preserves the trial as a printed legal record, making it a core source for comparing courtroom testimony with pamphlet narratives.
