# Source - Founders Online Braintree Association 1775

## Bibliographic Info
Founders Online/National Archives edition of the Braintree Committee report adapting the Continental Association for the town of Braintree, Massachusetts, on March 15, 1775.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- Braintree appointed a local committee to prepare a covenant consistent with the Continental Association.
- The town adapted the national Association into local rules on imports, consumption, exports, slave-trade discontinuation, sheep, domestic manufactures, pricing, and public enforcement.
- The Braintree text created one combined local committee from the town's committee of correspondence, committee of observation, and named residents including John Adams.
- The committee could publish violators in the Gazette and call for residents to break off dealings with them.
- The committee could inventory goods and, under certain conditions, take disputed goods into custody.
