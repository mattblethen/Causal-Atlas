# Source - Rhode Island Historical Society Burning of the Gaspee

## Bibliographic Info
Rhode Island Historical Society article on the 1772 burning of the Gaspee.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- The Gaspee was an armed British Navy ship active in Rhode Island waters.
- After grounding near Providence, it was attacked and burned by local colonists.
- RIHS frames the event as an early act of violence against British authority before the Boston Tea Party.
- The event connects customs enforcement, maritime trade, local resistance, and imperial investigation.
