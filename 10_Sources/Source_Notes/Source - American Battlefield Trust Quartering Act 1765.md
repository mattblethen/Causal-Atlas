# Source - American Battlefield Trust Quartering Act 1765

## Bibliographic Info
Primary-source presentation of the 1765 Quartering Act.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- The act required colonial officials to provide accommodations and supplies for British troops in America.
- The American Battlefield Trust notes the 1765 act did not authorize quartering in private homes.
- It made the cost and presence of the postwar army a colonial political issue.

## Relevant Quotes
