# Source - Wikisource Continental Association 1774

## Bibliographic Info
Public-domain transcription of the Continental Association adopted by the First Continental Congress on October 20, 1774.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- The Association framed colonial grievances as a system of administration adopted after 1763.
- It used economic sanctions and nonimportation as pressure against Britain.
- It linked revenue acts, trial concerns, Boston punishment, and Quebec Act anxieties.

## Relevant Quotes
> "ruinous system of Colony Administration"
