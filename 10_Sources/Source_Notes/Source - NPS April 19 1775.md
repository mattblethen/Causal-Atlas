## Bibliographic Info
National Park Service history page from Minute Man National Historical Park.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- April 19, 1775 marked the first armed conflict of the American Revolution.
- Fighting stretched across about sixteen miles from Boston toward Concord and back.
- British forces moved to seize and destroy arms and supplies at Concord.
- Massachusetts political organization and militia preparation had intensified after the Coercive Acts.
