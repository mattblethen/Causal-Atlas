# Source - UK National Archives Boston Tea Party Source 3

## Bibliographic Info
Letter by Lieutenant Colonel Alexander Leslie to Lord Barrington, December 17, 1773, catalogued by The National Archives.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- Leslie reported the destruction of 340 chests of tea on three ships the day after the Boston Tea Party.
- The letter identifies the Sons of Liberty as the likely actors in the British officer's account.
- Useful for showing how news of the event entered British military/government channels.

## Relevant Quotes
> "destroy'd 340 Chests of Tea"
