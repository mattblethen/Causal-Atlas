# Source - Avalon Circular Letter to Governors 1768

## Bibliographic Info
Avalon Project transcription of Lord Hillsborough's April 21, 1768 circular to colonial governors.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- The imperial ministry treated the Massachusetts Circular Letter as a dangerous attempt to coordinate colonial opposition.
- Governors were instructed to prevent colonial assemblies from endorsing or acting on the Massachusetts letter.
- If an assembly showed support for the letter, governors were told to prevent proceedings through prorogation or dissolution.

## Relevant Quotes
> "dangerous and factious tendency"
