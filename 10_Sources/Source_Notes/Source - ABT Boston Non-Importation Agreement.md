# Source - ABT Boston Non-Importation Agreement

## Bibliographic Info
American Battlefield Trust transcription of the August 1, 1768 Boston Non-Importation Agreement.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- Boston merchants and traders linked trade distress to customs duties, taxes, and restrictions imposed by recent Acts of Parliament.
- The signers promised to restrict imports from Great Britain and to avoid purchasing goods imported in violation of the agreement.
- The agreement targeted goods including tea, paper, glass, and painters' colours until the relevant duties were repealed.
- It used the language of industry, frugality, economy, and resistance to luxury.

## Relevant Quotes
> "promote industry, frugality, and economy"
