# Source - Avalon Virginia Committee Resolutions 1773

## Bibliographic Info
Transcription of the Virginia House of Burgesses resolutions establishing a committee of correspondence on March 12, 1773.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- Virginia framed the committee as a response to disturbances over ancient, legal, and constitutional rights.
- The House of Burgesses created a standing committee of correspondence and inquiry with eleven members.
- The committee was charged to obtain early and authentic intelligence about British acts, resolutions, and administrative proceedings affecting the colonies.
- The committee was instructed to maintain correspondence with sister colonies.
- The resolutions specifically asked about the Rhode Island inquiry with power to send accused persons overseas for trial.
