# Source - Massachusetts Archives Collection Overview

## Bibliographic Info
Massachusetts Archives overview of its early records collection, including the Provincial Period and Revolutionary Period.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- The Intolerable Acts changed the 1692 charter, closed Boston's port, and allowed quartering of British troops.
- In response, the General Court resolved itself into the first Provincial Congress on October 7, 1774, in Concord.
- The Provincial Congress created a Committee of Safety and gained legitimacy over most of Massachusetts outside British-controlled Boston.
