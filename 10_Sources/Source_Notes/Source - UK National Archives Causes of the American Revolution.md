# Source - UK National Archives Causes of the American Revolution

## Bibliographic Info
The National Archives (UK) collection story on causes of the American Revolution, including British government records.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- British policymakers connected postwar debt from the Seven Years' War to the belief that North American colonies should contribute more to imperial costs.
- Colonists had developed significant autonomy over nearly two centuries of British settlement.
- The source explicitly connects debt, autonomy, taxation, and colonial dissent.
- The National Archives flags that the story intersects with histories of slavery and Indigenous peoples, a useful reminder for module scope.
