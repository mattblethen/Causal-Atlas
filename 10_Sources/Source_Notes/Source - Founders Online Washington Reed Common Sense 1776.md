# Source - Founders Online Washington Reed Common Sense 1776

## Bibliographic Info
Founders Online/National Archives transcription of George Washington's April 1, 1776 letter to Lieutenant Colonel Joseph Reed.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- Washington wrote from Cambridge after the British evacuation of Boston and while preparing to move toward New York.
- He discussed the limits of reconciliation and the need for honorable terms.
- Washington reported that private letters from Virginia showed Common Sense changing many minds.
- Founders Online note 6 says Washington's view of the pamphlet's effect was based at least partly on Fielding Lewis's March 1776 letter, while Landon Carter denounced the pamphlet.

## Relevant Quotes
> "working a powerful change"
