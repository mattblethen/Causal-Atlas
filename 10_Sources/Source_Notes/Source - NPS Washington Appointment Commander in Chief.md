# Source - NPS Washington Appointment Commander in Chief

## Bibliographic Info
National Park Service article on George Washington's appointment as Commander in Chief of the Continental Army.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- Congress voted on June 14, 1775 to raise a standing army to aid the siege of British-controlled Boston.
- On June 15, 1775, Congress appointed George Washington Commander in Chief.
- John Adams favored a commander from Virginia to unify colonies beyond New England.
