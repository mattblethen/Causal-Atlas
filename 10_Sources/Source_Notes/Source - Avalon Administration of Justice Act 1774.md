# Source - Avalon Administration of Justice Act 1774

## Bibliographic Info
Text of the Administration of Justice Act, May 20, 1774, hosted by the Avalon Project at Yale Law School.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- The act concerned trials of persons acting to execute the law or suppress riots in Massachusetts.
- It allowed imperial authority to move some trials away from Massachusetts.
- Colonists saw the measure as weakening local accountability for imperial officials and soldiers.

## Relevant Quotes
> "impartial administration of justice"
