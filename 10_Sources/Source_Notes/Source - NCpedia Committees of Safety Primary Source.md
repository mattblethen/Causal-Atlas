# Source - NCpedia Committees of Safety Primary Source

## Bibliographic Info
NCpedia/ANCHOR primary-source collection excerpting North Carolina committee of safety minutes from 1775.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- North Carolina committees operated at town, county, and district levels.
- The source distinguishes committees of correspondence, inspection or observation, and safety.
- North Carolina committees helped implement trade boycotts endorsed by the Continental Congress and organized militia activity.
- Excerpts show committees policing horse racing, public dancing, imported goods, price violations, local manufacturing incentives, subscription to the Association, and obstruction of Boston relief.
- The collection links boycott enforcement to the weakening of royal government in North Carolina.

## Relevant Quotes
> "enforced violations of boycott resolutions"
