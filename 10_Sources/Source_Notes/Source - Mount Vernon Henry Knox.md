# Source - Mount Vernon Henry Knox

## Bibliographic Info
Mount Vernon digital encyclopedia article on Henry Knox.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- Henry Knox was a Boston bookseller and Continental Army officer.
- His "Noble Train of Artillery" brought cannon from Fort Ticonderoga to Boston.
- Knox later became a major general and served as the first U.S. Secretary of War.
