# Source - Army Museum Ticonderoga Provincials Seize the Fort

## Bibliographic Info
National Museum of the United States Army article on the May 1775 capture of Fort Ticonderoga.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- On May 10, 1775, colonial militia under Ethan Allen and Benedict Arnold seized Fort Ticonderoga.
- The fort was a British outpost at the southern tip of Lake Champlain.
- The raid delivered artillery and small arms that colonial troops besieging Boston badly needed.
- The source notes that some details remain debated, including particular statements and actions during the assault.
