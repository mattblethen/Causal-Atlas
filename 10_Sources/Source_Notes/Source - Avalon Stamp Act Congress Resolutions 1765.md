# Source - Avalon Stamp Act Congress Resolutions 1765

## Bibliographic Info
Avalon Project transcription of the Stamp Act Congress's October 19, 1765 resolutions.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- The resolutions frame colonial subjects as loyal to the Crown and subordinate to Parliament while also entitled to inherited British rights.
- They state that taxes require consent given personally or through representatives.
- They argue that colonial residents could not be represented in the British House of Commons because of local circumstances.
- They identify colonial legislatures as the only constitutional bodies able to impose taxes on colonists.
- They object to the Stamp Act and expanded admiralty jurisdiction as threats to colonial rights and liberties.

## Relevant Quotes
> "no taxes be imposed on them, but with their own consent"
