# Source - American Battlefield Trust Tea Act 1773

## Bibliographic Info
Primary-source presentation of the 1773 Tea Act.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- The Tea Act was passed on May 10, 1773.
- The act allowed customs drawbacks on tea exported to British colonies or plantations in America.
- The law empowered treasury commissioners to grant East India Company licenses to export tea duty-free from Britain.
- The policy advantaged East India Company tea in colonial markets while leaving the tea duty controversy politically alive.
