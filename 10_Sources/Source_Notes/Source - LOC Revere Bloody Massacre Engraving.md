# Source - LOC Revere Bloody Massacre Engraving

## Bibliographic Info
Library of Congress record for Paul Revere's Boston Massacre engraving, "The bloody massacre perpetrated in King Street Boston."

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- The print depicts British troops firing on civilians in Boston.
- The Custom House is labeled "Butcher's Hall," a clear interpretive choice.
- The print includes verse and lists those killed and wounded.
- The item is tied to Boston Massacre memory and political print culture.
