# Source - Commonwealth Museum Christopher Seider

## Bibliographic Info
Commonwealth Museum exhibit page on the death of Christopher Seider during the occupation crisis.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- Christopher Seider was a child of German immigrants whose exact name spelling is uncertain.
- Ebenezer Richardson was a Loyalist and informer connected to anti-smuggling enforcement.
- Richardson removed signs threatening merchants who imported British goods, then retreated home as a crowd followed him.
- Bird shot fired from inside Richardson's house killed Seider, who was not yet eleven.
- Samuel Adams and other Sons of Liberty organized a large public funeral.
- Seider's death became a symbol of British oppression and was soon overshadowed by the Boston Massacre eleven days later.
