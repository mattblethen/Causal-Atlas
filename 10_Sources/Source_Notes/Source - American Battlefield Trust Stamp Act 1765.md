# Source - American Battlefield Trust Stamp Act 1765

## Bibliographic Info
Primary-source text of the Stamp Act of 1765, presented by the American Battlefield Trust.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- The act imposed stamp duties on paper goods and legal/commercial documents in British America.
- Its preamble framed the measure as revenue for defending, protecting, and securing the colonies.
- The act made Parliament's fiscal authority directly visible in colonial daily and commercial life.
