# Source - LOC True Sons Non-Importation Broadside

## Bibliographic Info
Library of Congress catalog entry for a 1768 Boston broadside attributed to the Sons of Liberty.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- The item is a Boston broadside from 1768 connected to the non-importation agreement.
- It names the Sons of Liberty as the associated corporate author.
- It links the agreement to committees appointed by the body at Faneuil Hall and to printer support.
- The broadside shows the coercive and publicity-oriented side of boycott enforcement.
