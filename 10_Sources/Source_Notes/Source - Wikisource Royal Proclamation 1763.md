# Source - Wikisource Royal Proclamation 1763

## Bibliographic Info
Public-domain transcription of George III's Royal Proclamation of October 7, 1763.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- The proclamation followed the 1763 Treaty of Paris and organized newly acquired British territories.
- It created new governments including Quebec, East Florida, West Florida, and Grenada.
- It regulated western settlement, land grants, trade, and relations with Indigenous peoples.
- In the module, it helps connect the Seven Years' War to imperial administration, western expansion limits, and Indigenous sovereignty.
