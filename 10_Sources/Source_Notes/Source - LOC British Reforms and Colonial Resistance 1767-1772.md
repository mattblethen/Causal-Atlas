# Source - LOC British Reforms and Colonial Resistance 1767-1772

## Bibliographic Info
Library of Congress primary source timeline section on British reforms and colonial resistance from 1767 to 1772.

## Useful Claims / Evidence
- After the Stamp Act repeal, colonists still objected to British colonial policies.
- The Quartering Act of 1765 and the presence of a standing army created conflict.
- Britain disbanded the New York assembly in 1767 after it resisted mandated supplies.
- Colonists understood this as a threat that other assemblies could also be suspended.
