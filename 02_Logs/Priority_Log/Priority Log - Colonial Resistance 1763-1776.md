# Priority Log - Colonial Resistance 1763-1776

## 1763-1776 - Colonial Resistance Priority Stack

Priority Stack:
1. Defend local self-government and colonial assemblies.
2. Resist taxation without consent.
3. Preserve rights claimed as British subjects.
4. Coordinate resistance across colonies when imperial policy threatened more than one locality.
5. Escalate from protest to armed defense and independence when reconciliation failed.

Conflict:
Resistance often began as defense of older rights inside the empire, then moved toward revolutionary claims as coercion and armed conflict intensified.
