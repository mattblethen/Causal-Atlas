# Priority Log - British Empire 1763-1776

## 1763-1776 - British Empire Priority Stack

Priority Stack:
1. Stabilize and administer the expanded postwar empire.
2. Manage imperial debt and raise revenue for defense and administration.
3. Preserve Parliament's authority over colonies and trade.
4. Protect imperial commerce and favored institutions such as the East India Company.
5. Suppress resistance that threatened order or obedience.

Conflict:
Fiscal and sovereignty priorities pushed Britain toward policies colonists experienced as attacks on autonomy and consent.
