# Map Legend

Use maps to show relationships that are hard to see in prose.

## Link Types
- `Manifested From`: prior event, force, condition, or pressure that became visible.
- `Manifested As`: the form a force took in an event, policy, conflict, or institution.
- `Led To`: direct consequence, escalation, institutional reaction, or later event.
- `Related Decisions`: branch points chosen by actors or institutions.
- `Related Priorities`: actor priorities that explain why choices made sense at the time.
- `Related Threads`: long patterns that carry across modules.

## Map Folders
- `Causal_Maps`: event-to-event and force-to-event chains.
- `Pressure_Maps`: forces, constraints, incentives, and contradictions.
- `Actor_Maps`: people, groups, institutions, alliances, and conflicts.
- `Timeline_Zoom_Views`: nested timelines from broad eras to granular event chains.
