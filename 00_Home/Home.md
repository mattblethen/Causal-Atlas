# Causal Atlas

A zoomable Obsidian knowledge base for American and world history.

## Core Idea
History is represented as a system of:

- **Events** - what visibly happened
- **Forces** - deeper pressures that made events likely
- **Decisions** - conscious branches chosen by people or institutions
- **Priorities** - what actors were trying to preserve, gain, avoid, or transform
- **Threads** - long-running patterns that cross eras
- **Sources** - primary and secondary evidence supporting claims

## Prime Rule
Every major historical event should answer:

> What deeper pressure became visible here?

Example:

> The Boston Tea Party was not just destroyed tea. It was imperial debt, monopoly trade, colonial autonomy, and political symbolism becoming visible in Boston Harbor.
