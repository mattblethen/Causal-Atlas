# Committees of Safety

## Function
Committees of safety were local revolutionary bodies that helped enforce resistance measures, communicate resolutions, organize militia activity, and assume practical authority where royal government weakened.

## Priority Stack
1. Enforce boycott and Association commitments.
2. Coordinate local security and militia readiness.
3. Publicize committee decisions and pressure noncompliance.
4. Replace or bypass royal authority when imperial government lost local legitimacy.
