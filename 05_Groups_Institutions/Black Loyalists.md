# Black Loyalists

## Function
Black Loyalists were enslaved and free Black people who supported, joined, worked with, or fled to British forces during the Revolution, often seeking freedom from Patriot slaveholders.

## Priority Stack
1. Seek freedom and survival.
2. Exploit wartime openings created by British military policy.
3. Navigate danger from Patriot retaliation, British uncertainty, disease, and displacement.
