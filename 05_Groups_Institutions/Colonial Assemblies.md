# Colonial Assemblies

## Function
Colonial assemblies were local representative institutions that colonists treated as central to legitimate internal taxation and self-government.

## Priority Stack
1. Preserve local authority over internal taxation.
2. Defend colonial rights within the British constitutional order.
3. Coordinate with other colonies when imperial measures threatened shared interests.
