# British Customs Enforcement

## Function
British customs enforcement made imperial trade rules visible in ports, ships, seizures, duties, and prosecutions. In the Revolution module, it links fiscal policy to daily commercial conflict.

## Priority Stack
1. Enforce trade laws and customs duties.
2. Reduce smuggling and informal colonial trade.
3. Protect imperial revenue.
4. Support parliamentary authority in port economies.
