# Daughters of Liberty

## Function
The Daughters of Liberty were women who participated in colonial resistance through production, consumption choices, tea abstention, and public support for boycott culture.

## Priority Stack
1. Support colonial rights through everyday economic action.
2. Replace imported goods with local production where possible.
3. Make household consumption visible as political loyalty.
4. Expand resistance beyond formal male political institutions.
