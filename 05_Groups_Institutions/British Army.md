# British Army

## Function
The British Army enforced imperial authority and defended British control in North America. In the Revolution chain, it made parliamentary and royal authority physically present in Boston, Lexington, and Concord.

## Priority Stack
1. Maintain imperial order.
2. Protect British officials, posts, and military stores.
3. Disrupt provincial military preparations.
4. Avoid loss of control over Boston and Massachusetts.
