# East India Company

## Function
The East India Company was an imperial commercial actor whose financial distress and tea inventory became tied to British policy in 1773.

## Priority Stack
1. Sell tea and stabilize company finances.
2. Use imperial policy to protect commercial advantage.
3. Move tea into colonial markets.
