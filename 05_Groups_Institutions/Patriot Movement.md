# Patriot Movement

## Function
The Patriot movement coordinated and justified resistance to imperial policy, gradually shifting from rights claims inside the empire toward independence.

## Priority Stack
1. Resist taxation and coercion without consent.
2. Preserve liberty and self-government claims.
3. Build intercolonial solidarity.
4. Mobilize public opinion.
5. Sustain armed resistance after 1775.
