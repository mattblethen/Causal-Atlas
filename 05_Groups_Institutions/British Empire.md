# British Empire

## Function
The British Empire organized sovereignty, military protection, trade regulation, and revenue extraction across Britain and its colonies. In the American Revolution module, it is the system trying to manage a larger post-1763 empire while preserving parliamentary authority.

## Priority Stack
1. Preserve imperial sovereignty and obedience.
2. Pay for defense and administration after the Seven Years' War.
3. Regulate colonial trade for imperial advantage.
4. Maintain order in British North America.
5. Prevent resistance from becoming a general imperial challenge.
