# British Parliament

## Function
British Parliament legislated for the empire and asserted authority to tax and regulate the colonies. In the Revolution chain, Parliament is the institution through which debt pressure, trade policy, and sovereignty claims became law.

## Priority Stack
1. Assert legislative supremacy across the empire.
2. Raise or protect revenue for imperial costs.
3. Regulate trade and customs enforcement.
4. Punish defiance when imperial authority was challenged.
5. Preserve constitutional order as Parliament understood it.
