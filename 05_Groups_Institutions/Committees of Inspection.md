# Committees of Inspection

## Function
Committees of inspection were local enforcement bodies that watched compliance with the Continental Association, publicized violations, and helped turn boycott agreements into community-level governance.

## Priority Stack
1. Enforce nonimportation, nonconsumption, and related trade restrictions.
2. Identify and publicize violations of the Continental Association.
3. Coordinate information across towns, counties, ports, and colonies.
4. Preserve resistance unity by making private trade conduct publicly accountable.
