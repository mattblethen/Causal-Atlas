# Massachusetts Provincial Congress

## Function
The Massachusetts Provincial Congress functioned as a provisional Patriot government after the Coercive Acts undermined ordinary colonial governance. It organized legitimacy, defense, and resistance outside royal authority.

## Priority Stack
1. Preserve Massachusetts self-government.
2. Coordinate defense and safety.
3. Maintain legitimacy outside British-controlled Boston.
4. Communicate with towns and the Continental Congress.
