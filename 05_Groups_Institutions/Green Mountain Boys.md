# Green Mountain Boys

## Function
The Green Mountain Boys were a militia force associated with Ethan Allen and the capture of Fort Ticonderoga.

## Priority Stack
1. Act quickly through local/regional militia initiative.
2. Challenge British military possession at Ticonderoga.
3. Convert frontier organization into Patriot military advantage.
