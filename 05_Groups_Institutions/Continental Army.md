# Continental Army

## Function
The Continental Army transformed local and provincial armed resistance into a continental military institution under congressional authority.

## Priority Stack
1. Defend the colonies after fighting began.
2. Give Congress a military instrument beyond local militias.
3. Unify regional war efforts.
4. Sustain the siege of Boston and later broader campaigns.
