# Massachusetts Militia

## Function
The Massachusetts militia represented local armed capacity outside direct British control. In the causal chain, militia mobilization turned political resistance into armed resistance at Lexington and Concord.

## Priority Stack
1. Defend local communities and military stores.
2. Preserve provincial self-defense capacity.
3. Respond quickly to British military movement.
4. Sustain armed pressure around Boston after April 1775.

## Internal Tensions
