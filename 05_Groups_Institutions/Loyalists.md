# Loyalists

## Function
Loyalists were colonists who supported Britain, opposed independence, rejected Patriot tactics, or saw their interests as safer inside the empire. They make the Revolution visible as a civil conflict as well as an imperial conflict.

## Priority Stack
1. Preserve allegiance to the Crown or British constitution.
2. Avoid disorder, confiscation, or Patriot coercion.
3. Protect property, office, community, family, or trade relationships.
4. In some cases, fight for or support British military efforts.

## Internal Tensions
Loyalists were not one social class or one ideology. Some were cautious moderates, some Crown officeholders, some ideological imperial loyalists, some enslavers, and some people choosing the side they believed best protected survival. Black Loyalists require their own note because their loyalty often centered on freedom seeking rather than Crown ideology.
