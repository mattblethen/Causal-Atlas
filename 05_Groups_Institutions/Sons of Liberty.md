# Sons of Liberty

## Function
The Sons of Liberty were a colonial resistance network associated with opposition to the Stamp Act and later tea resistance.

## Priority Stack
1. Resist taxation without consent.
2. Pressure officials, merchants, and tea agents.
3. Defend colonial liberty as they understood it.
4. Use public mobilization, print, and association to enforce resistance.
