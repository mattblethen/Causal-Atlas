# Benedict Arnold

## Historical Function
Benedict Arnold was one of the Patriot commanders associated with the capture of Fort Ticonderoga before his later treason made him a very different historical symbol.

## Priority Stack
1. Seize military opportunity on the northern frontier.
2. Secure artillery and strategic posts for Patriot use.
3. Gain command credibility in the early war.
