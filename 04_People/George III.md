# George III

## Historical Function
George III was the monarch to whom colonial petitions appealed and whose government treated escalating resistance as rebellion. In the causal chain, he represents the imperial sovereignty pole that colonial petitioning failed to move.

## Priority Stack
1. Preserve imperial authority.
2. Suppress rebellion.
3. Maintain the British constitutional order as he understood it.
