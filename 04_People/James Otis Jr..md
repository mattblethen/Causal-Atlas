# James Otis Jr.

## Historical Function
James Otis Jr. was a Massachusetts lawyer and assembly figure whose role in Stamp Act and Townshend resistance connected formal constitutional argument to intercolonial coordination.

## Priority Stack
1. Defend colonial rights under the British constitution.
2. Use assembly politics and public argument to resist parliamentary taxation.
3. Help coordinate Massachusetts resistance with other colonies.
