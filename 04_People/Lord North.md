# Lord North

## Historical Function
Lord North was Britain's prime minister during the Tea Act, Boston Tea Party crisis, Coercive Acts, and the move from imperial crisis into war.

## Priority Stack
1. Preserve Parliament's authority.
2. Stabilize imperial finances and commerce.
3. Restore obedience after colonial defiance.
