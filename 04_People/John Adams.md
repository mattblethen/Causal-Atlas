# John Adams

## Historical Function
John Adams was a Massachusetts delegate and independence advocate attached to the congressional movement from resistance toward separation.

## Priority Stack
1. Defend colonial rights and constitutional claims.
2. Move Congress toward independence.
3. Legitimize resistance through law, argument, and public justification.
