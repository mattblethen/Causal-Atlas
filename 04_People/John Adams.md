# John Adams

## Historical Function
John Adams was a Massachusetts lawyer, delegate, and independence advocate. His Boston Massacre defense work shows how legal process and revolutionary politics could coexist before the movement hardened into independence.

## Priority Stack
1. Defend colonial rights and constitutional claims.
2. Preserve fair legal process even for unpopular defendants.
3. Move Congress toward independence.
4. Legitimize resistance through law, argument, and public justification.
