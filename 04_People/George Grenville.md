# George Grenville

## Historical Function
George Grenville was associated with the postwar revenue logic that led to the Stamp Act. In this vault, he anchors the British policy move from imperial debt pressure to direct colonial taxation.

## Priority Stack
1. Raise revenue for imperial costs.
2. Make colonial defense more financially sustainable.
3. Assert Parliament's authority to legislate for the colonies.
