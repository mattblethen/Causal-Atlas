# Thomas Gage

## Historical Function
Thomas Gage was the British military and governing figure whose attempt to seize colonial stores at Concord triggered the fighting at Lexington and Concord.

## Priority Stack
1. Restore imperial order in Massachusetts.
2. Disrupt colonial militia preparations.
3. Enforce imperial policy while avoiding uncontrolled escalation.
