# John Dickinson

## Historical Function
John Dickinson represents the reconciliation branch inside colonial resistance. He helped draft the Olive Branch Petition and argued for resolving the imperial crisis without immediate independence.

## Priority Stack
1. Preserve colonial rights.
2. Seek reconciliation inside the empire as long as possible.
3. Maintain congressional unity among moderates and independence advocates.
