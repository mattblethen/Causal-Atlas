# Samuel Adams

## Historical Function
Samuel Adams was a Massachusetts Patriot organizer whose political life connected committees, correspondence, protest, and intercolonial resistance.

## Priority Stack
1. Defend colonial rights and self-government.
2. Coordinate resistance through committees and correspondence.
3. Turn local grievances into wider political mobilization.
