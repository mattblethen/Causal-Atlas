# Henry Knox

## Historical Function
Henry Knox made artillery and logistics decisive in the Boston campaign by moving captured cannon from Fort Ticonderoga to the Continental Army.

## Priority Stack
1. Solve the artillery shortage around Boston.
2. Build credibility as a military professional.
3. Turn captured material into operational advantage.
