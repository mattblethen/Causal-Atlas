# 1767-1768 - Letters from a Farmer in Pennsylvania

## Summary
John Dickinson's Letters from a Farmer in Pennsylvania translated the Townshend duties into a widely circulated constitutional argument about taxation, representation, and imperial authority.

## What Happened
Dickinson published the letters in 1767 and 1768 under the persona of a Pennsylvania farmer. The letters argued that Parliament could regulate imperial trade but could not use duties to raise colonial revenue without colonial consent. The argument circulated through newspapers and pamphlet publication, helping refine resistance to the Townshend Acts.
