# 1766-1775 - Homespun Movement

## Summary
The homespun movement made domestic production, clothing, tea abstention, and household purchasing part of resistance to British taxation and imports.

## What Happened
Women participating as Daughters of Liberty organized spinning gatherings and supported abstention from tea and imported luxuries. Homespun production mattered because nonimportation created a practical need for substitutes and a symbolic way to demonstrate support for colonial rights.
