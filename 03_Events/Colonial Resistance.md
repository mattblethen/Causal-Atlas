# 1765-1774 - Colonial Resistance

## Summary
Colonial resistance was a sequence of protests, arguments, boycotts, assemblies, and crowd actions against postwar imperial policy. It made colonial autonomy and intercolonial political coordination increasingly visible.

## What Happened
Resistance began with Stamp Act protests and continued through later disputes over taxation, trade regulation, military presence, and imperial punishment. Newspapers, pamphlets, petitions, assemblies, and crowd mobilization helped turn local objections into a shared political conflict.
