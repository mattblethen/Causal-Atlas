# 1770-03-05 - Boston Massacre

## Summary
The Boston Massacre was a violent confrontation between British soldiers and civilians in Boston. It made the standing-army problem and media battle over legitimacy visible.

## What Happened
British soldiers fired into a crowd in Boston on March 5, 1770. Colonial accounts and printed materials turned the event into evidence of imperial danger, while legal and political disputes over responsibility continued.
