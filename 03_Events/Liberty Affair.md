# 1768-06-10 - Liberty Affair

## Summary
The Liberty Affair was the customs seizure of John Hancock's sloop Liberty and the crowd resistance that followed. It connected Townshend enforcement, naval presence, merchant politics, and the case for troops in Boston.

## What Happened
British customs officers seized Hancock's sloop Liberty and ordered it moved toward HMS Romney. Romney's earlier arrival, impressment activity, and the customs board's presence had already heightened tensions. The seizure intensified conflict between Boston crowds and imperial customs officials.
