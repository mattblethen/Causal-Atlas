# 1776-03-17 - Evacuation of Boston

## Summary
The British evacuation of Boston ended the first major campaign of the war and gave the Patriot cause a major military success before independence was declared.

## What Happened
After the Continental Army fortified Dorchester Heights, British commanders decided to evacuate Boston. British troops and Loyalist refugees departed for Halifax, Nova Scotia, while the Continental Army entered Boston.
