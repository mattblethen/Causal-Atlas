# 1775-06-17 - Battle of Bunker Hill

## Summary
The Battle of Bunker Hill was the first major battle of the American Revolution. It made clear that the conflict after Lexington and Concord had become a serious military confrontation rather than a brief disturbance.

## What Happened
Colonial forces fortified heights near Boston, mostly on Breed's Hill in Charlestown. British forces attacked and took the position after intense fighting. The battle followed the siege of British-controlled Boston and made the cost of suppressing the rebellion more visible.
