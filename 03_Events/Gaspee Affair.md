# 1772-06-09 - Gaspee Affair

## Summary
The Gaspee Affair was the attack and burning of a British customs vessel in Rhode Island. It made maritime enforcement and local resistance part of the escalation toward Revolution.

## What Happened
The British customs schooner Gaspee ran aground while active in Rhode Island waters. Local colonists boarded, attacked, and burned the vessel. The incident triggered imperial concern and colonial attention before the Boston Tea Party.
