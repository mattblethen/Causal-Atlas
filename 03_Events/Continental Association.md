# 1774-10-20 - Continental Association

## Summary
The Continental Association was the First Continental Congress's economic pressure program against Britain. It made trade, consumption, enforcement committees, and intercolonial unity part of resistance strategy.

## What Happened
The First Continental Congress adopted the Association on October 20, 1774. It used nonimportation and related sanctions to pressure Britain over post-1763 imperial policy and the Coercive Acts.
