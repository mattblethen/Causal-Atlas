# 1775-07-05 - Olive Branch Petition

## Summary
The Olive Branch Petition was Congress's major reconciliation appeal after fighting had begun. Its failure made continued imperial membership less plausible and strengthened the path toward independence.

## What Happened
Congress signed the petition in July 1775 as an appeal to King George III. The National Archives notes that the king did not formally receive or respond to it; his August 23 proclamation declaring the colonies in rebellion effectively answered it.
