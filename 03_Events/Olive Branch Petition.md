# 1775-07-05 - Olive Branch Petition

## Summary
The Olive Branch Petition was Congress's major reconciliation appeal after fighting had begun. Read alongside the July 6 Declaration of the Causes and Necessity of Taking Up Arms, it shows Congress justifying armed resistance while still leaving a formal path to imperial reconciliation.

## What Happened
Congress adopted the petition on July 5, 1775, and representatives signed it on July 8 as an appeal to King George III. Founders Online identifies John Dickinson as the author of the committee draft and notes that Congress adopted the petition with minimal amendment. The National Archives notes that the king did not formally receive or respond to it; his August 23 proclamation declaring the colonies in rebellion effectively answered it.
