# 1773-12-16 - Boston Tea Party

## Summary
The Boston Tea Party was the destruction of East India Company tea in Boston Harbor. It made taxation, monopoly trade, colonial resistance, and imperial authority visible in a single dramatic act.

## What Happened
On December 16, 1773, colonists boarded tea ships in Boston Harbor and destroyed 340 chests of East India Company tea. A British military account from the next day reported that the Sons of Liberty had destroyed tea aboard three ships.
