# 1775-04-19 - Siege of Boston

## Summary
The Siege of Boston was the first major campaign of the war, lasting from Lexington and Concord to the British evacuation in March 1776. It made the Revolution a sustained military and civilian crisis.

## What Happened
After Lexington and Concord, British forces held Boston while colonial forces surrounded the town. The siege involved Bunker Hill, harbor conflict, raids, supply scarcity, civilian suffering, and eventually Washington's use of Dorchester Heights.
