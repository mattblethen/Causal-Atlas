# 1774-09-05 - First Continental Congress

## Summary
The First Continental Congress brought delegates together in Philadelphia to respond to the Coercive Acts and wider imperial grievances. It made colonial resistance more formal, intercolonial, and institutionally durable.

## What Happened
The First Continental Congress met from September 5 to October 26, 1774. Delegates coordinated responses to imperial policy and created a precedent for continental political action outside Parliament.
