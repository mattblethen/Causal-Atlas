# 1775-05-10 - Second Continental Congress

## Summary
The Second Continental Congress became the central political authority for colonies already at war. It made colonial resistance into continental wartime governance.

## What Happened
The Second Continental Congress began meeting on May 10, 1775, after Lexington and Concord. It inherited a crisis already in motion: militia surrounded Boston, Britain treated resistance as rebellion, and delegates had to coordinate war, petition, finance, diplomacy, and legitimacy. In July it adopted both the Olive Branch Petition and the Declaration of the Causes and Necessity of Taking Up Arms, making the tension between reconciliation and armed resistance explicit.
