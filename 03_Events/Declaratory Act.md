# 1766-03-18 - Declaratory Act

## Summary
The Declaratory Act preserved Parliament's claim to full authority over the colonies even as the Stamp Act was repealed. It made the sovereignty conflict explicit.

## What Happened
Parliament declared that the colonies were subordinate to the crown and Parliament and that Parliament could bind the colonies in all cases whatsoever.
