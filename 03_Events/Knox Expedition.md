# 1775-11-17 - Knox Expedition

## Summary
The Knox Expedition moved captured artillery from Fort Ticonderoga to the Boston siege lines. It made logistics, not only battlefield courage, decisive in the early Revolution.

## What Happened
Henry Knox traveled to Fort Ticonderoga and helped move heavy artillery across difficult winter terrain toward Boston. The guns gave Washington's army the firepower needed to threaten British positions from Dorchester Heights.
