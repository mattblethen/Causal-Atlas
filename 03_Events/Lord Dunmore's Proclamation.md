# 1775-11-07 - Lord Dunmore's Proclamation

## Summary
Lord Dunmore's Proclamation offered freedom to enslaved people held by rebels if they joined British forces. It made slavery, military manpower, Black agency, Loyalism, and Patriot vulnerability visible inside the Revolution.

## What Happened
Virginia's royal governor John Murray, Earl of Dunmore, declared martial law and offered freedom to enslaved people held by rebels who were able and willing to bear arms for British troops. The proclamation alarmed slaveholding Patriots and created a path, however dangerous and limited, for enslaved people to seek freedom through British lines.
