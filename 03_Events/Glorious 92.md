# 1768-06-30 - Glorious 92

## Summary
The "Glorious 92" were the Massachusetts representatives who refused to rescind the Massachusetts Circular Letter. Their vote turned an assembly procedure into a symbol of resistance.

## What Happened
Lord Hillsborough demanded that Massachusetts withdraw its circular letter to other colonies. The Massachusetts House rejected the demand by a vote of 92 to 17. Governor Francis Bernard then closed the legislative session.
