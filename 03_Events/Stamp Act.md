# 1765-03-22 - Stamp Act

## Summary
The Stamp Act imposed duties on printed and legal materials in the colonies. It made Britain's postwar fiscal pressure and Parliament's claim to tax the colonies directly visible in ordinary legal, commercial, and media life.

## What Happened
Parliament passed the Stamp Act on March 22, 1765. The act required stamped paper for many documents and publications in British America. Its own language tied the revenue to the expense of defending and securing the colonies. Colonial opposition framed the measure as taxation without consent through colonial legislatures.
