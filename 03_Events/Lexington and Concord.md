# 1775-04-19 - Lexington and Concord

## Summary
Lexington and Concord were the first armed conflict of the American Revolution. They made the imperial crisis military: British enforcement and colonial militia preparation collided in open violence.

## What Happened
British regulars marched from Boston toward Concord to seize or destroy colonial military supplies. Militia mobilized across the countryside. Fighting occurred at Lexington, Concord's North Bridge, and along the British retreat route toward Boston.
