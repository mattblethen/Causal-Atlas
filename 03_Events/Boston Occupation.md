# 1768-1770 - Boston Occupation

## Summary
The occupation of Boston put British soldiers into the daily civic, economic, and political life of the town. It transformed abstract fears of a standing army into visible checkpoints, quartering conflicts, and street friction.

## What Happened
British warships entered Boston Harbor in late September 1768, and troops including the 14th and 29th Regiments landed soon after. Soldiers were quartered in public and improvised spaces, including Faneuil Hall, the Town House, Boston Common, and commercial buildings. The occupation produced disputes over housing, desertion, military punishment, checkpoints, and competition between soldiers and local workers.
