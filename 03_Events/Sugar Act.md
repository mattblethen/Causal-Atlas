# 1764-04-05 - Sugar Act

## Summary
The Sugar Act was an early postwar revenue and trade-enforcement measure. It made the fiscal and customs side of imperial reform visible before the Stamp Act.

## What Happened
Parliament revised duties on sugar, molasses, and other goods and strengthened customs enforcement. The act connected revenue to the expense of defending and securing the colonies.
