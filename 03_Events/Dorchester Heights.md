# 1776-03-04 - Dorchester Heights

## Summary
The fortification of Dorchester Heights forced the British evacuation of Boston. It made artillery, geography, and planning decisive in ending the siege.

## What Happened
Washington ordered Continental forces and local volunteers to fortify Dorchester Heights with cannon during the night of March 4, 1776. The position threatened Boston and the harbor, leading British forces to evacuate on March 17.
