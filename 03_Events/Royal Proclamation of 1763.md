# 1763-10-07 - Royal Proclamation of 1763

## Summary
The Royal Proclamation of 1763 organized Britain's enlarged North American empire after the Seven Years' War and attempted to regulate western settlement. It made imperial administration, settler expansion, and Indigenous sovereignty part of the postwar crisis.

## What Happened
George III issued the proclamation after the Treaty of Paris. It created new imperial governments, regulated western settlement and land grants, and tried to stabilize Britain's postwar relationship with Indigenous nations and colonists.
