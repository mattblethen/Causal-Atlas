# 1765-1775 - Nonimportation Agreements

## Summary
Nonimportation agreements turned trade, consumption, reputation, and public enforcement into tools of colonial resistance.

## What Happened
Colonial merchants, organizers, and local committees used agreements not to import certain British goods during the Stamp Act and Townshend crises. The strategy was meant to pressure British merchants and manufacturers, who might then pressure Parliament. Enforcement relied on public commitment, print, committees, merchant cooperation, and social pressure against defectors.
