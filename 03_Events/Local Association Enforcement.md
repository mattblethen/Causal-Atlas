# 1774-1776 - Local Association Enforcement

## Summary
Local Association enforcement turned the Continental Association from a congressional agreement into town, county, and city practice. Committees watched goods, prices, public conduct, signatures, manufacturing, and reputation.

## What Happened
After the Continental Association took effect, local committees adapted and enforced it. Braintree created a local covenant with a committee empowered to observe residents, publish violators, inventory goods, and break commercial ties. North Carolina committee minutes show enforcement against public balls, imported dry goods, price violations, noncompliance, and reluctance to support Boston relief. New York's Committee of Observation published proceedings as broadside evidence of local political authority.
