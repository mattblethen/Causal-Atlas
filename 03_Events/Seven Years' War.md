# 1756-1763 - Seven Years' War

## Summary
The Seven Years' War was a global imperial conflict that ended with Britain greatly expanded in North America but burdened by the cost of victory. In the American Revolution chain, the war made imperial debt, military administration, and postwar colonial control visible.

## What Happened
Britain, France, Spain, and their allies fought across Europe, North America, the Caribbean, West Africa, and Asia. The 1763 Treaty of Paris ended the conflict among Britain, France, Spain, and Portugal and confirmed a major shift in imperial control, especially in North America.
