# 1765-03-15 - Quartering Act

## Summary
The Quartering Act required colonial support for British troops in America. It made the cost and presence of the postwar army a political issue for colonial assemblies.

## What Happened
Parliament required colonial authorities to provide quarters and supplies for British forces. Colonial resistance, especially in New York, tied military administration to assembly autonomy and later parliamentary discipline.
