# 1768-08-01 - Boston Non-Importation Agreement

## Summary
The Boston Non-Importation Agreement was a local merchant boycott against British imports during the Townshend crisis. It showed how trade distress, taxation, and political resistance merged in Boston.

## What Happened
Boston merchants and traders agreed to restrict imports from Great Britain and to avoid buying goods imported in violation of the agreement. The agreement singled out goods tied to Townshend duties, including tea, paper, glass, and painters' colours, and described frugality and economy as civic practices.
