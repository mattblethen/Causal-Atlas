# 1770-02-22 - Christopher Seider Killing

## Summary
The killing of Christopher Seider turned nonimportation enforcement and Loyalist resistance into a public mourning crisis shortly before the Boston Massacre.

## What Happened
Ebenezer Richardson, a Loyalist and informer, removed signs threatening merchants who imported British goods. A crowd followed him home and attacked the house. Bird shot fired from inside killed Christopher Seider, a child not yet eleven. Samuel Adams and other Sons of Liberty organized a large funeral, and Seider's death became a symbol of oppression.
