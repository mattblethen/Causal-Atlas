# 1775-05-10 - Capture of Fort Ticonderoga

## Summary
The capture of Fort Ticonderoga gave Patriot forces artillery and opened a northern military dimension shortly after Lexington and Concord.

## What Happened
Colonial forces associated with Ethan Allen, Benedict Arnold, and the Green Mountain Boys surprised and seized the British post at Fort Ticonderoga. The captured artillery later became crucial to the Boston siege.
