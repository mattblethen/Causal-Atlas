# 1768-02-11 - Massachusetts Circular Letter

## Summary
The Massachusetts Circular Letter was an assembly-backed effort to coordinate colonial responses to the Townshend Acts. It made intercolonial communication itself a target of imperial suspicion.

## What Happened
The Massachusetts House sent a circular letter to other colonial assemblies arguing that revenue duties imposed by Parliament violated colonists' constitutional rights because colonists were not represented in Parliament. The letter urged the assemblies to coordinate. Lord Hillsborough then instructed colonial governors to block support for the Massachusetts letter and to dissolve assemblies if necessary.
