# 1776-07-04 - Declaration of Independence

## Summary
The Declaration of Independence announced the colonies' separation from Great Britain. It made the imperial crisis a formal claim of peoplehood, rights, self-government, and political rupture.

## What Happened
On July 4, 1776, the Second Continental Congress adopted the Declaration of Independence. The document announced separation from Great Britain and circulated through print, including broadside versions.
