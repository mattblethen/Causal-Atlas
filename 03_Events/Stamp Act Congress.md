# 1765-10-07 - Stamp Act Congress

## Summary
The Stamp Act Congress was an intercolonial meeting in New York that converted local Stamp Act resistance into a shared constitutional petitioning strategy.

## What Happened
Delegates from nine colonies met in New York in October 1765. They petitioned the king and Parliament and adopted resolutions arguing that colonists retained the rights of English subjects, that taxation required consent, and that colonial legislatures were the constitutional taxing bodies for colonists.
